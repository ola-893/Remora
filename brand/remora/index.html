01 / STORY

Ride the signal.

Ride a smart agent’s decisions by the second, without handing it your wallet.

In the ocean, a remora follows a stronger swimmer and feeds on what its hunt stirs up. Here the leader earns for publishing a decision, and followers pay for the signal they use.

Smart agents decide, cheap agents do, and the limits aren’t up to the model. Followers can run without a model of their own.

The leader records and publishes a move to a gated feed.

02 / ACCESSA stream pays for access

An onchain USDC stream pays by the second while the follower subscribes.

Local guards check the move. Wallet execution prompts the owner for approval.
